VON Evolution 2026 · Open evaluation workbench

Evaluate the conversation, not only the model

Real-time voice agents are systems.

A fluent transcript can hide interruptions, late tools, stale business state, and broken handoffs. Reliable evaluation preserves what happened across the entire interaction—and makes it reproducible.

The blind spot

One score cannot describe a live call.

ConversationWas the response relevant, safe, and grounded in what the caller actually said?
Real timeWere turn boundaries, latency, interruption, and recovery acceptable?
ExecutionDid tools complete correctly and did authoritative business state change?

The evaluation unit is the trace linking audio, timing, model decisions, tool evidence, and the final outcome.

Portable evidence

A vCon is a portable conversation container. The workbench adds versioned evaluation artifacts without pretending those application conventions are part of the core standard.

Open-source workflow

ConversationAgentEvals turns traces into regression evidence.

1 · NormalizeBring media, transcripts, timing, and tool observations into a consistent evidence model.
2 · EvaluateRun deterministic checks first, then use semantic judges only where interpretation is required.
3 · CompareRetain artifacts and compare releases by scenario, failure mode, and system component.

The takeaway

Make every claim traceable.

Evaluate the experience, the timing, the execution, and the final state. Keep the evidence portable. Then a “better” voice agent becomes a result you can inspect, reproduce, and trust.
